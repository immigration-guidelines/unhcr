Assessment
----------
UNHCR publishes a large body of real guidance for decision-makers: the Handbook and the Guidelines
on International Protection are the standard interpretive texts applied by asylum authorities and
courts, the Procedural Standards are the working manual for UNHCR's own mandate RSD, and the
country Eligibility Guidelines / International Protection Considerations are written expressly for
adjudicators. This folder now holds the country-guidance series (fairly completely for 2002-2026 in
English, as far as ecoi.net has it), but none of the thematic instruments, because every host that
serves them refused this machine. They should be added from a browser or another network
(addresses in not-obtained.tsv).
